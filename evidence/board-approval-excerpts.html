COMMITTEE REPORTS 
 A. Legislative Update – Jerrilynn Gundrum 
Mrs. Gundrum said there is a bill in the house committee, HB 411, which proposes to 
increase the minimum teacher’s salary to $50,000.00.  She encouraged every to read 
about this bill. 
 B. Butler Tech – Brian Begley 
Mr. Begley stated that the Butler Tech superintendent has turned in his resignation and a 
search will begin for his replacement. 
C. Student Achievement – Abby Berding-Miller 
Mrs. Berding-Miller highlighted the four Fairfield elementary schools who earned the 
2025 Best Elementary Schools badges from U.S. News & World Report.  The schools 
were West Elementary, North Elementary, South Elementary and Compass Elementary. 
D. Parks and Recreation – Scott Clark 
Mr. Clark said this Saturday at the Community Arts Center, the Festival of Trees is there 
which are decorated by organizations in the community.  Also, the Parade of Lights 
which runs from Water Works to Village Green and begins at 6:00 p.m.  Mr. Clark also 
mentioned the Elisha Morgan Mansion is decorated for Christmas and Harbin Park’s 
new playground opened last Saturday.   
 E. Planning Commission – Billy Smith 
Mr. Smith said the Mr. Robertson stated there was one item on the agenda at the last 
meeting for a request to develop a residential community on S. Gilmore Road for 
Whispering Hill PUD (formerly Benzing Estates) for the construction of 68 owner-
occupied paired villas.  Mr. Robertson was not in attendance at this meeting but will 
update as he learns more.
