Bus-by-bus reconciliation

How to read the comparison: The District described FAIRFIELD TRADES as containing estimated trade/scrap values and information supporting the disposal method or value considered. These are District-provided estimates, not verified appraisals. “SCRAP” is a label with no dollar estimate. Recycling averages divide the full batch payment by the number of buses; they are not documented individual sale prices. The settlements used combined weight. History-entry counts show how many extracted lines feed the summaries, not the number of distinct repairs or invoices.
